# Header Hub: Professional HTTP Header Management with Zero-Backend Privacy.


## Why Header Hub?

The Problem: > Most header managers force your sensitive rules and session data onto their proprietary clouds, creating a "black box" for your development secrets.

Our Solution: > Header Hub changes the game. We provide a professional-grade modification engine with zero third-party servers. Your rules, your infrastructure, your peace of mind.


## Key Features:

🚀 **High-Performance Engine:** Built on Chrome's latest declarativeNetRequest API (Manifest V3). Experience lightning-fast header modification without the latency of older, intercepting extensions.

🛡️ **Absolute Privacy (Zero-Backend):** No sign-ups, no tracking, and zero data collection. Your configurations never touch our servers—because we don't have any.

🔄 **Enterprise-Grade Git Sync:** Seamlessly synchronize your rules across devices or teams.

Works with GitHub, GitLab, and Gitea.

**Pro Tip:** For maximum security, we officially recommend using on-premise Gitea or GitLab instances. Keep your dev-ops data entirely within your team's private network.

🛠️ **Developer First:** Effortlessly manage Cookies, User-Agents, and Custom Auth headers. Bypass CORS restrictions or simulate different environments with a clean, intuitive UI designed for your daily workflow.

⚖️ **Compliance & Security**: Fully compliant with modern browser security standards. All sensitive tokens for Git sync are stored securely in your browser's local storage.
